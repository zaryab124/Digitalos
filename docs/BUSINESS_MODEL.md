## 1. Local Economic Reality of Jampur & South Punjab

Regional economies like Jampur, Rajanpur, and D.G. Khan possess robust underlying cash velocity driven by agriculture (cotton, wheat, sugarcane, mangoes) and bustling traditional bazaar retail. However, high platform fees charged by international aggregator apps (20-30%) fail in these markets because local merchant profit margins are narrow (8-15%).

**Jampur Digital OS** deploys a **low-take-rate, high-volume community utility model** that shares value with local stakeholders rather than extracting it.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               JDOS VALUE-SHARING ECONOMIC FLYWHEEL                                 |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

|             ▲                                         │                                           |

```

```
| 1. Local Commerce Take-Rate        | 1.5% to 3.0% per order     | Retail merchants, pharmacies    |

| 4. Delivery Platform Fee Split     | 20% of delivery fee        | Logistics fulfillment           |
| 5. Agri Bulk Trade Brokerage       | 0.5% of bulk grain trades  | Grain dealers, cotton ginners   |
| 6. Sponsored Local Ads & Banners   | PKR 1,500 - 5,000 / week   | Seed brands, clinics, schools   |
| 7. Job Board Featured Vacancies    | PKR 500 / listing          | Private schools, hospital clinics|
+------------------------------------+----------------------------+---------------------------------+
```

### Breakdown of Key Streams:

#### A. Micro-Commissions on Retail Commerce (1.5% – 3%)
- Rather than demanding 25% commissions (which alienates shopkeepers), JDOS charges a negligible 2% transaction processing and tech fee.
- Example: On a PKR 2,000 grocery order, JDOS receives PKR 40.

#### B. Delivery Fee Logistics Split (80/20 Rule)
- Standard intra-city delivery fee in Jampur: **PKR 100 – 150**.
- **80% (PKR 80–120)** goes directly to the Rider to ensure dignified daily wages.
- **20% (PKR 20–30)** retained by the Platform for maps, server routing, and emergency rider fund.

#### C. Merchant Subscription Tiers (Freemium)
- **Standard (Free Forever)**: Basic directory listing, up to 25 catalog products, standard search ranking.
- **Business Pro (PKR 999 / month)**: Unlimited catalog, "Verified Local Merchant" badge, priority search placement, weekly analytics report, WhatsApp catalog integration.
- **Enterprise (PKR 2,499 / month)**: Multi-branch management, bulk CSV inventory sync, dedicated account manager.

#### D. Agri Machinery & Direct Farm Trade
- Flat PKR 100 / day listing fee for tractor, harvester, and tube-well rental owners.
- Zero commission on basic farmer-to-farmer knowledge exchange.

## 3. Local Payment Gateway & Settlement Infrastructure

Given local banking penetration, the payment engine supports a **hybrid digital + cash** settlement matrix:

```
                                  PAYMENT METHODS
                                         │
        ┌────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                ▼                                ▼
[Cash on Delivery (COD)]       [Raast P2M / Instant QR]       [Mobile Wallets]
- Rider collects cash          - Zero transaction fees        - JazzCash Direct API
- System digital ledger        - State Bank instant clearing  - EasyPaisa Direct API
- Reconciled at end of shift   - Direct merchant bank credit  - 1Link 1Bill vouchers
```

### Daily Cash Settlement Protocol:
1. Riders operating on COD maintain a refundable security deposit or float ceiling (e.g. PKR 5,000).
2. Daily app reconciles COD collected vs. delivery payouts.
3. Balance settled seamlessly via JazzCash/EasyPaisa or Raast instant transfer at 9:00 PM daily.
